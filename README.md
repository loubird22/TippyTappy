Service Information Platform
A personal project exploring how human service organisations can move beyond activity reporting and better understand demand, complexity and change over time.

Most systems tell us WHAT using poor definitions that dont connect to reality

Providing little insight into profile of complexity and demand... and how effective the interaction from users is. Most social impact modeling needs love in practice. I'm tired of seeing folk gatekept out of reporting requirements and it holds them back from doing the important stuff like Aboriginal Data Sovrienty and progressive trauma informed care. So I reckon. Stop whinging and learn so it can be better maybe.

Core Idea
Capture operational information once and derive many uses from it.
Rather than building reports directly from cases and contacts, the platform generates reusable service profiles that work across service areas, funding obligations and domains

Need domains
Complexity factors
Service intensity,Episode patterns
Return and resolve rates

WHY I DO THIS ON WEEKENDS??
Many community and homelessness services work with fragmented systems, spreadsheet extracts and reporting requirements that focus heavily on activity rather than understanding.
A really junk attempt from a frustrated human services worker to create a stable intelligence layer from unstable operational data.

Concepts:
Episode-based service models Presenting units (individuals, households and families), Demand fingerprinting, Complexity and acuity modelling, Workforce and resource planning to consider children and culmulativerisk 
Information governance and auditability
Record lifecycle management and archival readiness
Multiple logics and interpretations via
Flexible reporting architectures
Status
Early-stage architecture and proof-of-concept.

Currently focused on data structures, service modelling and information architecture rather than user interface and interpretation. We'll get there.
